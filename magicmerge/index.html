Nine emoji lines, twelve levels each

🎁→ 🧸→ 🪄→ 💎→ 🌟→ 👑

Drag two identical items together to unlock the next one — a simple gift box climbs all the way to a cosmic crown. Merge quickly for a combo multiplier, and when a neighbour of the same kind fires on its own you get a chain ×3.

  • An enchanted garden

    Artifacts unlock buildings in a living isometric scene you can rotate and zoom. Upgrade to five stars, expand the garden to 5×5, trade spares at the altar. Every building grants a permanent bonus.

  • Orders, cards, an album

    Lily, Root and Saffi place orders right on the board — fill one and get paid on the spot. Lucky moments drop collectible cards: thirty-six in six sets, and a finished album opens the next volume.

  • Something on every day

    Lily's Kitchen, Root's Grotto, Saffi's Hide and Seek, Night Fireflies and the daily Sprint — with a themed event each week.

    All of it works with no connection and no account.
