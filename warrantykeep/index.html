  • Snap the receipt, skip the typing

    Photograph a receipt and on-device scanning fills in merchant, date and total. Pick a coverage length and the reminder is set. Email receipts and PDFs come straight in from Mail, Photos or Safari.

  • The payoff is the claim

    Warranty apps usually stop at storage — a folder of photos nobody opens again. When a deadline nears this one opens a ready-to-send claim: merchant, date, amount and the receipt photo together, then files it as resolved.

    That is the only reason anyone keeps a receipt at all.

  • Nothing leaves the device

    Works with no connection and no account — nothing to sign up for, nothing to be locked out of. Find any item from Spotlight, or just ask Siri what's expiring.
