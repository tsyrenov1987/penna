What the computer is given, and what it is not

The bot receives

  • Its own hand
  • The cards on the table
  • The discard pile
  • The trump suit and how many cards are left

The bot never receives

  • Your hand
  • Any other player's hand
  • The order of the undealt deck
  • A second look before it commits a move

And you can check it: the deal number is shown on the result screen, every hand is revealed when the game ends, and any game can be replayed move for move.

  • Both the rules people argue about

    Throw-in (podkidnoy) and transfer (perevodnoy), two to four at the table. Four levels of opponent, from Novice to a Master who actually calculates the game instead of playing at random.

  • Eight opponents with a temper

    Mikhalych, Sanyok, the Professor, the Kid — and the critters: Barsik, the Owl, the Beaver and the Fox. They sulk when they lose, gloat when they win, and tell jokes in between.

  • Online, without an account

    A duel on two phones: the server matches you by rating in seconds. Elo, epaulette points and a leaderboard — and a table you can open by code or by link for someone you know.

    No ads, no sign-up, and no watch-a-video-for-a-hint.
