Illustrative values. In the app the reading is computed for your date and place, with the haircut guidance the tradition actually gives for each lunar day.

  • Calculated, not guessed

    Dates follow specific Pugpa-school methods documented in printed Buryat almanacs, and are verified against them. The app says plainly that it is cultural and educational — not fortune-telling.

  • The classical readings

    Compatibility runs the full four-parameter analysis: five Mahabhuta, twelve Zhil, eight Parkha, nine Menge. A birth chart gives your animal, element, Menge mark and Suudal direction.

  • More than a calendar

    A Shamatha timer and nine-round purification breathing. A twelve-lesson introduction to Buddhism with quizzes, a twenty-term glossary, and biographies of seventeen teachers.

    Maps and details for forty datsans across Russia, Mongolia, Tibet, India, Nepal, the USA and Europe.
