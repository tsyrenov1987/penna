The hard part was never knowing what to do. It's not feeling the hour go past.

  • See time

    An estimated duration on every tile — 15, 30, 45, 60 minutes or your own. A countdown ring on the task you're doing right now, and a widget that says what's now, what's next and how long is left.

    The morning check-in takes three taps: how much energy you have today, and the day rearranges to fit it.

  • AI that never leaves the phone

    Magic Breakdown turns a task too big to start into 3–7 first steps, each under fifteen minutes. Organize sorts your Brain Dump into dated, tagged tiles — you approve every change. Smart input reads “call mom tomorrow evening.”

    Runs through Apple Intelligence on device. Needs iPhone 15 Pro or newer; everything else works on any iPhone with iOS 18.

  • Pay once. Nothing leaves.

    No subscription, ever. No account, no servers, no ads, no trackers. Face ID lock on individual notes, AES-256 encrypted backups free forever, export to Markdown, JSON, PDF or calendar.

    The App Store privacy label reads Data Not Collected, and it's literally true.

What Penna deliberately isn't

  • ✕Not a subscription. The category runs on monthly billing. Pro is one purchase and it stays bought.
  • ✕Not a cloud app. There is no account to make and no server to trust. Your day lives on your phone.
  • ✕Not a productivity scoreboard. No streak shaming, no completion percentage staring at you. An unfinished day just moves.
  • ✕Not another to-do list. A list hides time. A board shows it — which is the whole reason this app exists.
