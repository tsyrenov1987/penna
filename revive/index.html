What a generative model does

Reads the damaged face, then paints a plausible new one over it. The result is sharp, confident and slightly not her. As a keepsake it is worthless.

What Revive does

Heals the pixels that survived. Colour is laid over structure that never shifts. A crease is mended where the crease actually is, and nowhere else.

  • Repair, not invention

    Noise, dust, scratches and creases removed; faces repaired, never redrawn. Colourise a black-and-white frame naturally or vividly — your call. Upscale to 4× without losing the grain that makes it old.

    Before/after slider and full-resolution export on every result.

  • Nothing leaves the phone

    Every neural network runs on your iPhone. Revive does not upload, works with the aeroplane mode on, and cannot train on your pictures — it never has them.

    Privacy by construction rather than by promise.

  • A whole album, not one frame

    Batch Restore takes the shoebox at once. A Library keeps every restoration. Magic Eraser lifts a stain or a stranger out of the frame, and Animate turns a still into a slow parallax clip.

    Print scanner straightens a paper photo shot with the camera.
