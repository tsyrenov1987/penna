  • Scan, don't type

    A receipt, a VIN barcode, the odometer, an OBD trouble code — all read on device and filled in for you. That is the difference between a log you keep and a log you abandon.

  • It knows your car

    Oil capacity, lug-nut torque and maintenance intervals for 100+ popular cars, stored offline — useful in a garage with no signal. Official NHTSA recalls, ratings and known issues too.

    An on-device assistant answers from your own log: what's overdue, what you've spent.

  • Proof at resale

    One tap produces a buyer-ready PDF service history. Lifetime and per-mile cost in clear charts, warranty and registration expiry tracked, and a private digital glovebox for the documents.

    Your data stays yours and exports whenever you want it.
